WatchDraw

Draw right on your Galaxy Watch — no phone needed.

WatchDraw is a standalone Wear OS drawing app built for the round Galaxy Watch display. Open the app and sketch directly on your wrist, with smooth strokes, a color palette, brush sizes, and haptic feedback on every action.

Features

  • Drawing canvas with smooth, fluid strokes
  • Curated color palette and three brush sizes
  • Undo / redo with two-finger gestures
  • "My draws" gallery, Favorites, and a Trash with restore
  • Export as an image: save to your gallery or share
  • Optional: sign in with Google to sync your drawings to your own Google Photos

Google Photos sync

When you tap Download on a drawing, WatchDraw can upload that single image to your own Google Photos library (using the photoslibrary.appendonly scope), so you can view and share your drawings on your phone. The app only adds images it creates — it never reads, lists, or browses your existing photos, and there is no server in between. Signed out, everything stays local on the watch.

Privacy & contact

We don't sell or share your data. Your drawings are stored locally on the watch; the optional Google Photos sync goes only to your own account. Read the full Privacy Policy.
